About

Samuel A. Church is a reporter and a member of the Harvard College Class of 2027, studying Social Studies. He is from Austin, TX. He spent the summer of 2026 on the national team at Bloomberg News, reporting data-driven stories on politics, higher education and public spending. He is returning to Bloomberg as a full-time reporter after his graduation in May.

At The Harvard Crimson, he covered Harvard College, including its fight against the Trump administration, pro-Palestine activism on campus, and the College's move to recenter academics. In 2025, he was a summer reporting fellow at The Provincetown Independent through the Local Journalism Project, where he covered funding cuts and municipal institutions on the Outer Cape.
