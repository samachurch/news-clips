About

Samuel A. Church is a reporter and a member of the Harvard College Class of 2027, studying Social Studies. He spent the summer of 2026 on the national desk at Bloomberg News, the team's sole intern, reporting data-driven stories on politics, higher education and public spending.

At The Harvard Crimson, he covered Harvard College, including the University's fight against the Trump administration, pro-Palestine activism, and move to recenter its academics. In 2025 he was a summer reporting fellow at The Provincetown Independent through the Local Journalism Project, covering funding cuts and municipal institutions on the Outer Cape.

He is from Austin, Texas.
